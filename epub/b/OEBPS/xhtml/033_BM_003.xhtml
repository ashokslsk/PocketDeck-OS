ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

While the actual writing of a book is often a solitary activity, its images and ideas necessarily emerge from a rich network of influences. Naming every one of these influences would clearly be impossible, but I do wish to acknowledge—with deep bows of gratitude—the following people:

My mother, whose lifelong interest in nutrition and t’ai-chi to support health and well-being sowed fertile seeds.

James MacRitchie, Eva Wong, Charles Chase, and Li Junfeng, for inspiring ch’i-kung instruction.

Francis Lucille, for nourishing and clarifying my passion for nondual spiritual inquiry, which provides the spark for creative endeavors such as this one.

And finally, deepest appreciation for mystic poets—past, present, and future—whose playful-serious speaking of the unspeakable remains an eternal source of inspiration.
