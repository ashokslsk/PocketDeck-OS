Reading fonts for EPUB/TXT books (copy this "fonts" folder to the SD card root).

NotoSansCJK/  Japanese, Chinese and Korean books (also Latin), sizes 8/10/12/14.
              Converted from Noto Sans CJK SC (SIL Open Font License 1.1) with
              lib/EpdFont/scripts/fontconvert_sdcard.py. Select it in
              Settings > Reader > Font Options > Font Family (or per book).
              For Japanese glyph shapes you can also download "Noto Sans JP"
              in Settings > Reader > Font Options > Download Fonts.

STIXMath/     Math, physics and science books: STIX Two text with STIX Two Math for
              Greek letters, arrows and math symbols (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
              Sizes 12/14/16/18. Choose it per book: Menu > Book Options >
              Font Options > Font Family > STIXMath.

Kannada books do not need a font here: they use /tools/fonts/kannada.knf.
Spanish and Russian books use the built-in fonts.
Any .cpfont family folder can be added here or in /.fonts/.
