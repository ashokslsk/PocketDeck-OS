English dictionary for the reader (copy this "dictionaries" folder to the SD card root).

WordNet/   Princeton WordNet 3.0, 147,306 English words and phrases (StarDict format),
           with lookup accelerators (.idx.oft, .idx.oft.cspt) already built.
           WordNet is free to use and redistribute under the WordNet License:
           https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use

More dictionaries (StarDict format, one folder each, never nested twice):
  /dictionaries/<Name>/<Name>.ifo, .idx, .dict   (or /.dictionaries/)
Prepare compressed downloads (.dict.dz) first, with
  python3 scripts/dictionary_tools.py prep <folder>
or online with Inky: https://inky.crossink.dev/#dictionary-tools
Choose the active dictionary in Settings > Reader > Dictionary.
